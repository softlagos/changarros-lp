Vujadmin — Privacy Policy

Effective date: May 3, 2025

1. Introduction

ASV Software ("we", "us", or "our") operates Vujadmin, a business administration tool for mobile devices. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect information when you use our application.

2. Information we collect

Vujadmin may collect the following types of information:

  • Camera access: The app requests access to your device's camera. This is used solely to enable in-app functionality such as scanning documents or capturing photos for business purposes. We do not store or transmit camera images to our servers without your explicit action.
  • Location data: The app may request access to your device's location while the app is in use (foreground only). This is used to show nearby businesses and related locations relevant to your work. We do not store or share your location data.
  • In-app messages: Vujadmin includes an in-app messaging feature for communication between users. Messages are transmitted securely to facilitate real-time communication. We do not store message content beyond what is needed for delivery, and we do not share messages with any third parties.
  • Account information: Information you provide when registering or using the app, such as your name and email address.
  • Usage data: Basic information about how you interact with the app, used to improve performance and user experience.

3. How we use your information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Provide and maintain the functionality of Vujadmin
  • Show nearby businesses and location-relevant content while you use the app
  • Enable real-time in-app communication between users
  • Improve and personalize your experience
  • Respond to support requests or inquiries
  • Ensure the security and integrity of the application

4. Camera permission

Vujadmin requests the CAMERA permission on Android devices. Camera access is used only when you actively use a camera-dependent feature within the app. We do not access your camera in the background, and no images are shared with third parties without your knowledge.

5. Location permission

Vujadmin requests access to your device's location while the app is actively in use (foreground only). Location data is used exclusively to display nearby businesses and related points of interest relevant to your tasks. We do not collect, store, or share your location data with any third parties.

6. In-app messaging

Vujadmin provides an in-app messaging feature to allow communication between users within the platform. All messages are transmitted securely. We do not retain message content after delivery, and we do not share any message data with third parties under any circumstances.

7. Data sharing

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information to third parties. We may share data only with trusted service providers who assist in operating the app, under strict confidentiality agreements.

8. Data retention

We retain your personal information only for as long as necessary to provide our services or as required by law. You may request deletion of your data at any time by contacting us.

9. Security

We take reasonable measures to protect your information against unauthorized access, loss, or misuse. However, no method of transmission over the internet or electronic storage is 100% secure.

10. Children's privacy

Vujadmin is not intended for use by children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify users of any significant changes by updating the effective date at the top of this page.

12. Contact us
